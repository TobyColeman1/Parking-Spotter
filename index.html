      Where the data comes from. Street parking is the 795 Dublin City Council pay-and-display meters (June 2025 list), with zone prices from the council’s 2022 zone map and paid hours from its 2021 meter list. Outside the city it covers Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown’s 344 meters (positions from a 2018 survey, current fees), Fingal’s 139 meters (one usual rate, €1.20/h, as the council doesn’t publish prices per meter) and 78 South Dublin paid streets. Car park positions are approximate and their prices aren’t published as data. Live car park spaces come from Dublin City Council when the site is online. Rules change, so always check the sign or meter.

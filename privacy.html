Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Parking Spotter (parkingspotter.net) is a free website that helps you find parking in Dublin. It is run by Toby Coleman, a sole trader based in Ireland, who is responsible for your information under the GDPR. In short: there are no accounts, no cookies and no advertising, and we never see what you type or where you are.

What we collect

We count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly service that uses no cookies. For each visit it records the page, the website you came from, your browser type, screen size and rough country. It does not store your IP address.

We also count a few anonymous actions so we know which features are useful: searching, using your location, tapping the map, choosing a quick pick, choosing a custom stay, opening a result and reporting wrong information. Only the name of the action is sent. What you typed, the place you picked and your location are never sent.

We use this to improve the site, which is our legitimate interest under the GDPR. None of it identifies you.

Your location

If you tap “Use my location”, your browser asks your permission first. Your position is used only inside your browser to find parking near you. It is not sent to us.

Other services the site uses

Like any website, these services receive your IP address and basic browser details when your browser contacts them:

  • Address search: what you type in the search box goes to Photon (run by Komoot) and, as a backup, Nominatim (run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation), so they can find the address.
  • Map: map images come from OpenStreetMap, which can see which part of the map you are looking at.
  • Code library: the map code loads from cdnjs, run by Cloudflare.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps short-term server logs for security. Netlify is based in the US and uses the EU’s approved safeguards for data transfers.
  • Live car park spaces: these come from Dublin City Council through our own server. Nothing about you is sent to the council.

Saved on your device

If you save a place or report wrong information, it is stored in your browser’s own storage on your device. It is not sent to us, and you can clear it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Because we don’t collect anything that identifies you, we usually won’t hold anything. Email info@parkingspotter.net with any question. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland.

Changes

If we change how the site uses information, for example by adding accounts, we will update this page and the date at the top.
